What We Do

Core Expertise

Secondary Processes

🔩

How We Work

Our Streamlined Process & Lead Time

1
Within 24 Hours

Inquiry & RFQ

Send us your 3D drawings (STEP/IGS). Our sales team will provide a detailed commercial quote and preliminary DFM feedback swiftly.

2
3 – 5 Days

Mold Design & DFM

Before steel cutting, we issue a comprehensive DFM report, optimizing parting lines and wall thickness to eliminate potential molding defects.

3
15 – 25 Days

Mold Fabrication

High-precision tool steel cutting, CNC machining, and EDM to match rigorous tolerances.

4
3 – 5 Days

T1 Samples & Approval

We perform the first trial run (T1) and dispatch physical samples via DHL/FedEx for dimensional inspection.

5
7 – 14 Days

Production & Finishing

High-efficiency mass manufacturing starts. Secondary surface treatments like printing or painting are executed seamlessly in-house.

6
1 – 3 Days

Sub-Assembly & Final OQC

100% cosmetic checks and functional verification before final seaworthy packaging.
