About Us

Malaysia’s Trusted Plastic Injection Moulding Partner Since 1988

Industri Nikkoplas Sdn. Bhd. is an ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certified precision plastic injection moulding manufacturer based in Johor Bahru, Malaysia. We have supplied quality plastic components to leading consumer electronics, telecommunication, and industrial clients for over three decades.

Our 28-machine fleet (50T–450T) processes PS, ABS, PC, PP, and engineering resins with tight tolerances. Every part is backed by in-house secondary processing — spray painting, silk screen printing, hot stamping, pad printing, and ultrasonic welding — under one ISO-controlled roof.

Our Johor Bahru facility sits within the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ), giving OEM and Tier 1 buyers streamlined cross-border logistics, tax incentives, and easy access to the Singapore supply chain and port network — alongside Malaysia’s competitive manufacturing cost base.

Secondary Processing

In-House Finishing & Assembly

All secondary processes run within our Johor Bahru facility — no outsourcing, no handoffs, complete traceability from moulding to finished part.

Silk Screen Printing

Multi-colour screen printing on flat and mildly curved plastic surfaces ±0.1 mm registration.

Pad Printing

Transfers ink onto irregular, concave, and convex plastic surfaces where screen printing cannot reach.

Spray Painting

Gloss, matte and texture coatings. Colour-matched to Pantone or OEM colour chips.

Hot Stamping

Metallic, holographic, and matte foil decoration applied with heat and pressure. No inks, no VOCs.

Ultrasonic Welding

Permanent hermetic joints between thermoplastic parts — no adhesives, no fasteners, sub-second cycle time.
