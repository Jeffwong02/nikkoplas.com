Our Story

35+ Years of Manufacturing Excellence

Industri Nikkoplas Sdn. Bhd. was established in 1988 in Johor Bahru, Malaysia, and has spent more than 35 years serving global industrial partners as a precision plastic injection moulding manufacturer.

Based in Tampoi, Johor Bahru, our facility operates 28 horizontal injection machines from 50T to 450T, alongside in-house secondary processing and sub-assembly lines — giving our partners a single, reliable source for molded components through to finished, ready-to-ship sets.
