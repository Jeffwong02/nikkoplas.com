The Problem With Multi-Vendor Finishing

Many small-to-mid molders subcontract painting, printing, and welding to separate finishing shops. Every handoff adds shipping time, a second QC checkpoint where defects can be introduced, and one more vendor relationship to manage and audit.

When color matching or a cosmetic finish is critical, a mismatch discovered after the part has already left the mould shop means a return trip, a delay, and a difficult conversation with your own customer.

  • No shipping parts between separate moulding and finishing vendors
  • No second QC handoff where damage or defects go unnoticed
  • One point of contact and one production schedule to track
  • Faster turnaround from mould to finished, decorated part

What's Actually In-House

Every secondary process runs inside our ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certified facility, on the same site as moulding production:

  • Spray painting — gloss, matte, texture, Pantone/OEM colour-matched
  • Silk screen printing — multi-colour, ±0.1mm registration
  • Pad printing — for irregular, concave, and convex surfaces
  • Hot stamping — metallic, holographic, and matte foil decoration
  • Ultrasonic welding — permanent joints, no adhesives or fasteners
  • Sub-assembly — multi-part builds delivered ready to install
