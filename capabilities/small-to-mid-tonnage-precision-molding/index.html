Small-to-Mid Tonnage
Precision Injection Moulding, 50T–450T

We don't chase automotive-scale tooling or micro-parts. Our 28-machine fleet is built and tuned for the tonnage range where consumer electronics, telecom, and appliance components live — with the tight tolerances that range demands.

Why We Specialize in This Range

Large-tonnage automotive moulders (600T+) are built around big parts and long production runs. Micro-moulders are built around tiny, high-precision electronic components measured in grams. Neither is optimized for the range in between — small-to-mid tonnage parts that need precision, consistent cosmetic finish, and moderate-to-high mix production flexibility.

That's the range we've built around: 28 machines from 50T to 450T, tuned for exactly the parts consumer electronics, telecom, and appliance OEMs actually need moulded.

  • 28 horizontal injection moulding machines, 50T–450T
  • Tolerances to ±0.05mm on critical dimensions
  • Single-cavity to multi-cavity and family tooling
  • PS, ABS, PC, PP, and engineering resin capability
  • DFM review and First Article Inspection before steel commitment

What Fits This Range

If your part fits a 50T–450T press, you're in our specialization, not a general-purpose fit. Typical parts in this range include:

  • Consumer electronics housings and bezels
  • Telecommunication equipment enclosures
  • Home appliance panels and structural components
  • Connector housings and terminal blocks
  • Industrial enclosures and control panel housings
  • Cable management brackets, clips, and mounting hardware
Is This the Right Fit?

Know Before You Reach Out

We'd rather you find the right supplier the first time than wait through a quote cycle that isn't a fit.

Good Fit For Us

  • Parts requiring 50T–450T clamping force
  • Consumer electronics, telecom, or appliance components
  • Cosmetic parts needing in-house paint, print, or hot stamp
  • Multi-cavity or family tooling for efficient mid-volume runs
  • Projects needing ISO 9001:2015-documented quality control

Better Served Elsewhere

  • Large automotive structural parts (600T+ tonnage)
  • Ultra-high-volume commodity parts at rock-bottom unit cost
  • Sub-gram micro-precision electronic components
  • Metals, die-casting, or non-plastic processes
