What We Do

Core Capabilities

Materials

We specialise in high-performance resins including PS, ABS, and PC, along with other engineering plastics selected to match your component's mechanical and thermal requirements.

Machine Range

28 horizontal injection moulding machines from 50T to 450T — giving us flexibility from small precision components to larger structural parts. See our Machinery page for the full fleet breakdown.

DFM Analysis

Before any steel is cut, we issue a full Design for Manufacturability (DFM) report addressing parting lines, wall thickness, and other factors — helping you avoid costly redesigns after tooling has started.

Tooling & IP Protection

All molds remain the client's property after tooling payment. We provide routine maintenance and secure storage. Tooling is never used for third-party production. NDAs available before any drawings are submitted.

Secondary Processing

In-house silk screen printing, pad printing, spray painting, hot stamping, ultrasonic welding, and laser engraving — no outsourcing required. See Secondary Processes.

Sub-Assembly

Multi-part grouping and component assembly — combining multiple moulded parts into complete, ready-to-ship sets. Simplify your supply chain with one trusted partner.
