Plastic Injection Moulding

ISO 9001:2015 certified plastic injection moulding and finishing services for electronics, telecommunication, and engineering-grade OEM buyers — produced in Johor Bahru, Malaysia.

Electronic, Telecom & Engineering-Grade Moulding

Electronic and telecommunication plastic components demand tight dimensional tolerances, consistent material properties, and rigorous quality documentation. Industri Nikkoplas has supplied plastic housings and engineering-grade structural components to electronics and telecom OEMs for over two decades from our Johor Bahru facility.

Our 28 injection moulding machines (50T–450T) handle the range from small connector and clip-fit components to medium-sized equipment enclosures, all running under our ISO 9001:2015 quality system.

  • Tolerances to ±0.05 mm on critical dimensions
  • ABS, PC/ABS, PP, Nylon (PA6, PA66), and glass-filled engineering variants
  • Tool validation with First Article Inspection (FAI) reports
  • DFM review before steel commitment
  • In-house secondary processing (spray paint, silk screen, hot stamp)
  • Sub-assembly of multi-component plastic assemblies

Components We Produce

We produce both cosmetic and structural plastic components for electronic, telecommunication, and engineering applications:

  • Consumer electronics housings and bezels
  • Telecommunication equipment enclosures
  • Connector housings and terminal blocks
  • PCB brackets, standoffs, and mounting components
  • Cable management brackets and clips
  • Precision engineering-grade structural parts
  • Switch and control panel housings
Our Services for Electronics, Telecom & Engineering

End-to-End Plastic Manufacturing

From mould design review to finished, decorated, and assembled parts — all under one roof in Johor Bahru.

Precision Injection Moulding

28 horizontal machines from 50T to 450T. Multi-cavity and family tooling for cost-efficient electronics and telecom production runs.

Spray Painting & Texture Coating

Gloss, matte, soft-touch, and texture finishes. Colour-matched to Pantone or OEM colour chips. Adhesion tested to electronics and telecom industry standards.

Hot Stamping & Silk Screening

Decorative foil hot stamping and silk screen printing for branded trim elements, function labels, and logo details.

Sub-Assembly

Multi-part plastic assemblies using ultrasonic welding and snap-fit integration — delivered as ready-to-install modules.

DFM & Tooling Support

Design for Manufacturability review, tool qualification, PPAP-style first article inspection, and ongoing SPC monitoring.

ISO-Certified Quality

ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certified facility. Full traceability, incoming material inspection, and in-process QC records.
