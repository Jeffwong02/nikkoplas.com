ISO 9001:2015

The global benchmark for Quality Management Systems. Certifies that our processes — from order intake and material inspection through moulding, secondary processing, and final inspection — consistently deliver products meeting customer and regulatory requirements.

Environmental Management

Certifies that Industri Nikkoplas operates an Environmental Management System that identifies, monitors, and reduces the environmental impact of our manufacturing operations — including waste handling, energy consumption, and chemical use.

Why It Matters to You

What Our Certification Means for Your Supply Chain

Consistent Dimensional Quality

ISO 9001 requires documented process controls and in-process inspection, meaning your parts hit tolerance specifications batch after batch.

Full Traceability

Every production lot is traceable from raw material certificate through moulding records to outgoing inspection — essential for consumer electronics and telecommunication audit requirements.

Corrective Action System

Non-conformances trigger a documented corrective action process. You receive 8D or equivalent root-cause analysis reports on request.

Environmental Compliance

ISO 14001 certification helps your sourcing team satisfy ESG reporting requirements and demonstrate responsible supply chain practices to your stakeholders.

Supplier Approval Ready

Dual ISO certification satisfies the supplier qualification criteria of global OEMs including Panasonic, Intretech, and BM Nagano — reducing your vendor approval timeline.

Continuous Improvement

ISO mandates annual internal audits and management reviews. Our quality KPIs are tracked monthly, and improvement objectives are set and measured every year.
