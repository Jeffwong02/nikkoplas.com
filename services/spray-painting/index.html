Spray Painting & Coating
for Plastic Components

In-house rotary spray painting on injection moulded plastic parts — gloss, matte, texture, and soft-touch finishes, run on either our automated or semi-automated rotary line depending on your volume and finish needs.

Choose Your Line

Auto Spray or Semi Auto Spray (Rotary)

Both lines run the same finishes, colour-matching standard, and ISO 9001:2015 quality control — the difference is throughput versus flexibility.

Auto Spray (Rotary)

Fully automated rotary spray line for medium-to-high volume production, where consistent film thickness and repeatable coverage matter most.
