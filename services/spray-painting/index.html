Spray Painting & Coating
for Plastic Components

Premium in-house spray painting on injection moulded plastic parts — gloss, matte, texture, and soft-touch finishes that meet stringent consumer electronics standards.

Precision Spray Painting on Plastic

Spray painting enhances the aesthetic and functional properties of injection moulded plastic components. Applied in controlled spray booths, it delivers uniform coverage, adhesion, and colour consistency across high-volume production runs.

Industri Nikkoplas operates dedicated spray painting lines in our Johor Bahru facility, running directly after moulding. Every painted batch is inspected under standardised lighting for colour consistency, surface defects, and adhesion.

  • Gloss, semi-gloss, satin, matte, and texture finishes
  • Soft-touch rubber coating for tactile premium feel
  • Metallic and chrome-effect coatings
  • 2-component (2K) paint for superior durability
  • Compatible with ABS, PC/ABS, PS, and PP substrates
  • Colour matching to Pantone, RAL, or customer colour chip

Typical Applications

Our spray-painted components are used across consumer electronics, home appliances, automotive interiors, and industrial housings:

  • Consumer electronics enclosures and bezels
  • Home appliance fascias and control panels
  • Automotive interior trim panels
  • Power tool housings
  • Medical device outer casings
  • Display stands and retail fixtures

Panasonic-approved colour and gloss standards are available for consumer electronics clients.

Finish Options

Available Surface Finishes

High Gloss

Mirror-like surface reflectivity. Ideal for premium consumer electronics and lifestyle product exteriors.

Matte / Satin

Low-sheen finish that hides fingerprints and minor surface imperfections. Popular for industrial and professional equipment.

Soft-Touch Rubber

Rubberised coating that delivers a premium tactile feel. Resistant to oil, sweat, and mild solvents.

Texture Coat

Sand or pebble texture patterns that mask sink marks, improve grip, and give a rugged engineered look.
