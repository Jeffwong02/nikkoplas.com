Service Overview

What Is Hot Stamping?

Hot stamping (also called foil blocking or hot foil printing) presses a heated die against a metallic or pigmented foil and onto the plastic surface, transferring a precise image in one clean step. No inks, no solvents — the foil bonds permanently to the substrate under heat and pressure, producing a durable, premium finish that elevates product appearance.

No Ink or Solvents

The foil transfer process is dry — no wet ink, no cure time, no VOC emissions. Faster cycle and cleaner production floor compared to paint-based processes.

Premium Appearance

Gold, silver, and holographic foils give branded parts a high-end, luxury look that significantly increases perceived product value on retail shelves.

Durable Bond

Heat-and-pressure bonding creates a mechanical adhesion to the plastic substrate — resistant to abrasion, moisture, and UV compared to surface inks.

ISO Quality Control

All hot stamping runs are checked under our ISO 9001:2015 system — foil adhesion peel test, edge sharpness, and positional accuracy verified per batch.
