In-house pad printing on curved, recessed, and irregular plastic surfaces — single and multi-colour, ISO 9001:2015 quality control.

Service Overview

What Is Pad Printing?

Pad printing transfers ink from an etched plate onto a silicone pad, then onto the part. Because the pad is flexible, it conforms to curved, concave, and uneven surfaces — making it ideal for logos, part numbers, and icons on moulded plastic components where flat-bed screen printing cannot reach.

Curved & 3D Surfaces

The flexible silicone pad conforms to any surface geometry — concave, convex, or deeply recessed areas that screen printing cannot access.

Fine Detail & Registration

Suitable for small logos, part numbers, icons, and text with high registration accuracy — important for consumer electronics and branded housings.

Multi-Colour Capability

Sequential colour passes allow multi-colour prints in a single pass-through — no need for a separate operation between colours.

ISO-Certified Quality

All pad printing runs under our ISO 9001:2015 QC system — ink adhesion, colour accuracy, and registration are verified before shipment.
