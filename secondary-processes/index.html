Secondary Processing Services
in Johor Bahru

Full in-house surface finishing, decoration, and joining — no outsourcing, no delays.

What We Offer

In-House Secondary Processes

All processes run on-site alongside our injection molding lines. Components move straight from molding into finishing — eliminating the delays and quality risk of an external subcontractor.

🖨️

Silk Screen Printing

Precision silk screen printing for logos, part numbers, warning labels, and decorative markings on plastic components. Suitable for single-color and multi-color print runs with tight registration tolerances.

🖊️

Pad Printing

Pad printing for curved, textured, or irregular surfaces where silk screen isn't practical — commonly used for small electronic housings, buttons, and control panel components.

🎨

Spray Painting

In-house spray painting lines for cosmetic finishing, including primer, base coat, and clear coat processes. Color matching available to customer specifications (Pantone / RAL references).

⚡

Hot Stamping

Foil-based hot stamping for metallic finishes, branding, and high-contrast markings that need to withstand handling and wear.

🔊

Ultrasonic Welding

Ultrasonic welding for joining plastic components without adhesives or fasteners — used in sub-assembly work where a strong, clean bond is required.

🔆

Laser Engraving

Precision laser engraving for permanent, high-resolution markings including serial numbers, QR codes, and fine detail graphics.

Why In-House Matters

Because all secondary processes run under one roof alongside our injection molding lines, we eliminate vendor handoffs and quality gaps. Parts move directly from molding into finishing — tighter control, faster throughput, lower risk for you.
